# One-Origin-Convergence-State-Wave-and-Consciousness
One Origin, Convergence, State, Wave and Consciousness  A Conceptual Research Model by Sanjay Soni  Author: Sanjay Soni Independent Researcher — Conceptual Science &amp; Philosophy
Abstract

This conceptual research model explores whether apparently separate phenomena such as state, interaction, wave, information, experience, and consciousness may be understood as different expressions or descriptions of an underlying process.

The model begins with a philosophical proposition: an extremely subtle fundamental origin may contain, in an inseparable form, the potential for what we describe separately as state, interaction, and wave. When examined from different perspectives, these aspects appear to be distinct, although they may represent different descriptions of the same underlying process.

This work is presented as an open conceptual hypothesis, not as an established physical theory.

---

1. The Fundamental Idea

The model proposes the following conceptual relationship:

One Origin → Convergence → Relationship → Change → Flow → New State

An individual entity may therefore be viewed not as a completely isolated object, but as the result of relationships and combinations occurring within a larger system.

In this sense:

«A state, an interaction, and a wave may be different observable or conceptual expressions of one underlying process rather than three completely independent fundamental entities.»

This proposition requires further mathematical and experimental investigation.

---

2. One Origin and Three Expressions

The model considers three concepts:

- State — the condition or configuration of a system.
- Interaction — the relationship through which systems influence or respond to one another.
- Wave — a propagating pattern or change that may carry energy, momentum, or information depending on the physical system.

Rather than treating these as necessarily independent foundations, the model asks whether they can be interpreted as different descriptions of a common underlying process.

This is a philosophical and conceptual proposal and should not be interpreted as a replacement for established physical definitions.

---

3. Convergence

The term convergence is used here to describe the coming together or relationship of multiple components.

Conceptually:

Origin → Convergence → Interaction → Change → Flow → Outcome

A new state may emerge from the relationships among components.

Thus, what appears to be a new independent structure may instead be an emergent configuration produced by many interacting components.

---

4. Information and Conscious Experience

The model further asks whether biological experience can be understood through the relationship between:

Signals → Information → Processing → Memory → Experience

The term “information index” (सूचनांक) is proposed as a future conceptual quantity for describing the informational condition available to a system.

At present, this term has no established universal physical definition.

Therefore, a future version of the model would need to define:

1. What constitutes information.
2. How information can be quantified.
3. How an information index could be calculated.
4. How it could be experimentally tested.
5. Whether such a quantity has predictive value.

The model does not claim that consciousness has been scientifically reduced to an information index.

---

5. Beginning and End as Observational Boundaries

A further philosophical question arises from the model.

When we observe a particular state, we often identify a beginning and an end.

However, a beginning may represent the result of an earlier process, while an apparent ending may become the condition for another state.

Therefore:

«Beginning and end may sometimes represent boundaries selected by observation rather than absolute boundaries of the complete process.»

This is a philosophical proposition and does not by itself establish that the universe or physical processes are infinite or cyclic.

---

6. The Point and the Ocean

A metaphor used in this model is:

«“A point exists within the ocean, and the ocean is expressed through countless points and their relationships.”»

The point represents a local or microscopic description.

The ocean represents the larger system.

The purpose of this metaphor is to illustrate that the microscopic and macroscopic descriptions may be deeply related, while recognizing that a metaphor is not a physical proof.

---

7. Proposed Conceptual Sequence

The central conceptual sequence is:

One Origin
↓
Multiple Convergences
↓
States
↓
Interactions / Waves / Flows
↓
Information
↓
Experience
↓
New State

The arrows represent conceptual relationships, not necessarily a universally established physical sequence.

---

8. Core Research Question

The central question of this model is:

«Are apparently separate phenomena truly independent at the fundamental level, or could they represent different expressions of an interconnected underlying process?»

This question may be explored through philosophy, physics, information science, biology, and consciousness studies.

---

9. Scientific Safety and Scope

This repository presents an independent conceptual research hypothesis.

It does not claim:

- a new experimentally confirmed physical law;
- a replacement for established physics;
- scientific proof of God or any religious doctrine;
- a scientifically established explanation of consciousness;
- proof that all physical phenomena originate from one specific entity;
- proof that the universe is necessarily eternal or cyclic.

The purpose is to document ideas, formulate questions, develop definitions, and invite critical examination, mathematical formalization, simulation, and future experimental testing.

---

10. Future Development

Future work may attempt to develop:

- mathematical definitions of the proposed concepts;
- a formal definition of “information index”;
- measurable relationships between state, interaction and wave;
- computational or simulation-based models;
- comparison with existing physical theories;
- biological applications;
- testable predictions;
- conditions that could falsify or modify the hypothesis.

A hypothesis becomes scientifically stronger when it produces clear definitions and testable predictions, including conditions under which the hypothesis could be shown to be incorrect.

---

Conclusion

This model proposes a philosophical possibility:

«What appears separate at one level of observation may be interconnected at a deeper level of description.»

State, interaction, wave, information, experience, and large-scale structure are therefore explored here not necessarily as completely independent foundations, but as potentially related expressions of an underlying process.

This work is intentionally presented as an open conceptual research model. It is offered for discussion, criticism, refinement, mathematical development, and possible future scientific testing.
